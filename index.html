What if waste stopped at the source, and material started there?

EcoFresh Building decentralised waste‑to‑valueinfrastructure for India

The problem

Waste disposal and materials production never meet.

India pays to get rid of its organic waste, while plastic is still made almost entirely from fossil fuels.

no link today

What we build

EcoFresh builds the link between waste and material.

  1. At the source

    A compact unit at each site prepares the waste on site, splitting it into sugar liquor and fat.

  2. Collected

    The prepared streams are picked up on a schedule, sealed and metered.

  3. At the hub

    One shared hub serves a cluster of sites and is designed to ferment both streams into PHA bioplastic.

  4. To market

    PHA powder goes to compounders and converters. Packaging brands drive the demand.

How a cluster works

One cluster is the building block. Five sites, one hub.

Schematic. Sites sit within about two kilometres of each other; the hub sits in the nearest industrial zone that permits it. Repeat the cell city by city.

Why this model

Decentralised where waste is made. Centralised where scale matters.

Option A

All centralised

  • ✕Raw, wet waste is trucked across the city and starts rotting within hours
  • ✕Sites keep paying to get rid of it, and still miss their on-site duty

Option B

All decentralised

  • ✕A full fermentation plant, operators and a pollution consent at every site
  • ✕Capital and permits multiply with every site added

EcoFresh

The split

  • ✓Disposal is solved at the source, where the law now asks for it
  • ✓The hub gets ready-made feedstock it would otherwise have to buy
  1. Disposal is a source problem

    Food waste rots within hours, and since April 2026 bulk generators must process it on site. So the messy step happens where it is made, and the waste leaves sealed, stable and pumpable.

  2. Feedstock is what makes PHA expensive

    The carbon source can represent more than half the overall production costs. Our hub ferments sugar liquor and fat that sites would otherwise pay to dispose of.

    Alvarez Chavez, Raghavan and Tartakovsky, RSC Advances, 2022
  3. Fermentation needs scale

    It needs trained operators, a pollution consent and large tanks, so it is done once, at one hub serving a cluster of sites.

Sites stop paying just to dispose. The hub stops buying feedstock. That difference is where EcoFresh earns.

The science of what gets left behind

Not every “bioplastic” goes back to nature.

One family is made by microbes, and eaten by microbes: PHA.

Breakdown speed depends on the environment, thickness and grade. Claims for specific products need testing against the relevant standard.

Meet the PHA family

Bacteria already make these plastics. Food waste can feed them.

PHA (polyhydroxyalkanoates) is not one material but a family of plastics that microbes build inside their own cells. Different members are stiff or flexible, depending on the microbe and what it is fed. EcoFresh is designing the system that turns everyday food waste into the food those microbes need.

The Indian mandate

India banned single-use plastic in 2022. Compostable plastic is exempt.

Every banned item now needs a certified compostable substitute. That is demand created by law, not by preference.

The entry ticket

CPCB certification, with products conforming to IS/ISO 17088.

Why institutional waste

Hotels, campuses, canteens. The best food waste in India, already in one place.

Already separated

Kitchens sort it at source. No plastic, glass or grit to pick out.

Steady, every day

Lunch and dinner, every day of the year. A feed you can plan a plant around.

Close together

Large hotels sit in clusters, so one hub can serve five sites on a short route.

Rich in starch and fat

The two carbon sources microbes turn into PHA, in high shares.

Mixed city waste

Dirty, variable and scattered. Hard to use as a clean feed.

And the law now pushes large generators to deal with their own waste: Solid Waste Management Rules 2026.

What's inside the waste

Two-thirds of it is carbon microbes can turn into PHA.

  1. 48%Starch + sugarssugar liquor → sugar train
  2. 19.7%Fatfat line → fat train
  3. 18.7%Proteinthe nitrogen microbes need to grow
  4. 13.6%Ash, fibre, otherpressed cake → compost

Published figures for institutional food waste. Hotel waste will differ; we will publish our own lab analysis when it is done.

Step one · at the site

A sealed unit turns scraps into two clean feeds. No one has to touch it.

No operator on siteSealed and odour-filteredSmaller than a parking spaceEvery litre metered

Step two · collection

Five sites, one hub. Tanks call for pickup before they fill.

Each unit pings the hub when its tanks are nearly full.

One route collects from all five on a planned schedule.

Liquor and fat travel apart, because each goes to its own train.

Step three · the hub

Two trains, one plant. Each microbe eats what it is best at.

Runs open, no steam steriliserNo pressure vesselsNo solventWater reusedShips as powder

Getting the PHA out

No chloroform. Water does the work.

No solvent extraction. Many PHA plants dissolve cells in chloroform. We don't.

The product leaves as PHA powder, ready for compounders and converters.

Nothing wasted

Every stream has a place to go. Even the heat.

  • Sugar and fat become PHA.
  • Pressed cake goes on to composting.
  • Water from the hub is designed to be reused, not discharged.
  • Heat the liquor already carries is put back to work at the hub.
  • Unused fat is skimmed off and kept, not lost.

Where EcoFresh fits

The pattern is complete for materials. EcoFresh is the local first step and the shared hub.

  1. Step 2 · Local first stepAt the source

    A compact unit at each site prepares the waste on site, splitting it into sugar liquor and fat.

  2. LinkCollected

    The prepared streams are picked up on a schedule, sealed and metered.

  3. Step 3 · Shared processingAt the hub

    One shared hub serves a cluster of sites and is designed to ferment both streams into PHA bioplastic.

  4. Step 5 · Market pullTo market

    PHA powder goes to compounders and converters. Packaging brands drive the demand.
